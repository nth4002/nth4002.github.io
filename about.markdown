I started this blog mainly to document what I learn.

When I encounter an interesting problem, make an engineering decision, or learn something useful while building a project, I want to write it down.

The goal ius simple: share practical knowledge from actual development work.

I don;t indent to make every post a complete tutorial. Some posts may explain a problem, the approach that I took, what worked, what didn't, and what I learned from it.
